# Translation notes (Brazilian Portuguese release)

Candidates found while reading `RISING.INI` (`NAME=` is the in-game name) and `TEXTES.TXT`. Nothing
here is changed yet: these are to be reviewed with the people who know the game. The section name is
the original French identifier; the French name tells what the unit or building really is.

| section (French) | meaning | current `NAME=` | note |
|---|---|---|---|
| `[VOLEUR]` | thief | `ARQUEIRO` (archer) | mistranslated; this is the unit that steals technologies and resources (the "archer" in the bug report) |
| `[OURS DE COMBAT]` | combat bear | `DELL'ORSO` | Italian; "Urso de combate" |
| `[SANGLIER]` | wild boar | `LANÇADOR` (thrower) | boar → "Javali"; maybe the English release renamed it, check the sprite |
| `[LEZARD]` | lizard | `RAPTOR` | maybe intentional |
| `[TORTUE]` | tortoise/turtle | `ESTALADOR` | ? |
| `[FOURMI]` | ant | `FOURMI` | not translated: "Formiga" |
| `[ARAIGNEE]` | spider | `ARAIGNEE` | not translated: "Aranha" |
| `[SUN]` | sun | `SUN` | ? |
| `[FOOTMAN]` | foot soldier | `BUKKA` | ? |
| `[RECOLTEUSE]` | harvester | `CEIFEIRO` | fine ("Ceifeira" for the machine) |

Also worth a pass: the menu strings in `TEXTES.TXT` (`// Languettes`, options, mission texts) and
`EVENEMENT` texts; `monde*.txt` are the mission briefings.

The in-game option "VELOCIDADE" (speed) exists twice in `TEXTES.TXT` (game speed and scroll speed).
